# cubegame-voxel-asset
1. Read Art Direction and the exact approved object/reference; verify provenance and rights.
2. Identify installed authoring tools and actual engine, geometry scale and pivots.
3. Produce one editable asset with front/side/three-quarter evidence and useful game-camera view.
4. Verify export and Unity import, materials, transform, collision and real lighting.
5. Submit source, export, screenshots, license notes and owner visual review status.
